# Changelog

Notable changes to Slate, newest first. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org):
**major** for changes that break saved data or backups, **minor** for new features, **patch** for fixes.

Add notes under **Unreleased** as you work. When you release, rename that heading to the
new version and date (see "Releasing" in the README).

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-07

First public release.

- This week: what needs deciding, slots used against target, and who can start when.
- Caseload with status tags, filters and one-click Invite back, Send chase and Release.
- Forecast of capacity over 1–26 weeks, with safe and best-case free slots.
- Waitlist with check-in reminders after 14 and 21 days.
- Editable message templates that fill in client, contact, date and your name.
- Guided four-step weekly review.
- Backup export and import, auto-save to a file (Chrome and Edge), optional PIN lock.
- Works offline and installs as an app on desktop, iPad and iPhone.
- In-app notice when a new version is available.
- Licence keys: try everything on sample data, then unlock with a key bought through Stripe. Unlocking needs internet once; after that Slate works offline.
